1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the Airline Reservation Management System (ARMS 2.0), whether as a registered passenger, administrative user, or guest visitor, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Terms of Service and applicable aviation laws.

2. Flight Inventory and Reservation Nature

Flight schedules, fares, and seat counts shown here are records stored in ARMS and are not independently verified or live airline-distribution offers. Submitting a request creates an internal ARMS reservation record and reference.

An ARMS status change or approval applies only to the record in this application. Unless ARMS is connected to and confirms a reservation with an airline or authorized booking provider, it does not constitute an airline ticket, confirmed carriage, or boarding pass. Verify travel arrangements directly with the relevant carrier.

3. Guest Mode and Self-Service Bookings

ARMS provides unauthenticated guest booking capabilities. When booking as a guest:

  • You must provide accurate passenger names exactly matching government-issued photo identification.
  • A unique, unguessable booking reference is generated for each transaction.
  • Guest booking lookup and management requires providing both the booking reference and verified passenger credentials (email address or surname).
  • You are responsible for safeguarding your booking reference code.

4. Cancellation and Modification Rules

Passengers may cancel reservations through self-service portals (My Bookings or Guest Booking Lookup) subject to the following rules:

  • ARMS self-service cancellation is subject to the status and departure time recorded in the application.
  • ARMS cancellation changes the application reservation record; it does not cancel an external airline booking unless that integration is explicitly configured and confirms the cancellation.
  • Seat availability shown after cancellation reflects the ARMS inventory.

5. AI Travel Assistant Disclosures

The ARMS AI Travel Assistant provides natural language travel discovery, itinerary suggestions, and policy guidance:

  • Flight numbers, departure times, fares, and seat counts reflect ARMS records, not independent airline confirmation or live distribution data.
  • Budget guidance, hotel spending estimates, and destination activity plans are simulated planning estimates and do not constitute fixed binding offers.
  • Passengers must verify visa, entry requirements, and health documentation with respective embassy authorities before departure.

6. Passenger Conduct and Security

Users must not attempt automated scraping, injection attacks, unauthorized seat hoarding, or credential enumeration. Violations will result in immediate termination of account access and cancellation of associated reservations.
