Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. Version 2.0

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Airline Reservation Management System (ARMS 2.0) collects, uses, protects, and retains passenger data across web portals, guest booking workflows, and AI travel interactions.

2. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization and collect only information necessary to provide airline reservation services:

  • Passenger Details: Full legal name, gender, contact phone number, and email address for booking confirmations.
  • Flight and Seat Preferences: Selected flight route, departure dates, seat class, and specific seat allocation.
  • Account Authentication: Hashed passwords, Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) verification timestamps, and security questions.
  • AI Travel Conversations: Temporary session interaction history used solely to provide contextual travel recommendations during your active visit.

3. Guest Mode and Data Minimization

Guest users are not required to create an account, provide payment card details, or submit unnecessary personal information. Guest booking records are isolated and accessible only via cryptographically unguessable references combined with secondary passenger verification.

4. Data Storage and Security Controls

We enforce strict technical and organizational safeguards:

  • All database connections and web traffic are protected by TLS encryption with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Passwords are hashed using industry standard PBKDF2 with SHA-256 signatures.
  • Concurrent seat bookings are protected by database row-level locking to prevent race conditions.
  • Rate limiting is enforced on authentication and guest lookup endpoints to prevent brute-force attacks.

5. Third-Party Integrations

ARMS connects with external aviation telemetry providers (including OpenSky Network and AviationStack) to display live flight radar positions. No passenger personal data is transmitted to external flight tracking providers.

6. Your Rights and Contact Information

Registered passengers can review, update, or delete personal preferences at any time via the Profile portal. For data access inquiries or account deletion requests, please contact privacy@arms-aviation.local.
